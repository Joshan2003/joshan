About Me

I am a passionate Renewable Energy Engineer currently pursuing my MSc Advanced Electrical & Electronic Engineering at the University of Nottingham — a Russell Group institution ranked Top 100 globally for Electrical Engineering.

My journey into renewable energy began at a 600 MW coal-fired power station in Tamil Nadu, India. Standing next to turbines burning coal, I thought — there has to be a better way. That thought never left me.

Since then I have independently designed solar-wind hybrid systems for remote health centres in the Libyan Sahara Desert, simulated commercial rooftop PV systems for UK university buildings, and conducted full LV network hosting capacity studies using industry-standard tools and grid codes.

I bring hands-on solar PV site engineering experience from Virupaksha Solar Energy — I know what happens between the design office and a live system.
