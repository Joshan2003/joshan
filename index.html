// 01 · ABOUT
The Engineer
Behind The Work

I am a passionate Renewable Energy Engineer currently pursuing my MSc Advanced Electrical & Electronic Engineering at the University of Nottingham.

"I did not plan to fall in love with renewable energy. It happened inside a 600 MW coal-fired power station in Tamil Nadu. Standing next to turbines burning coal, I thought there has to be a better way. That thought never left me."

Since then, every project I have taken on has been building that better way. I have independently designed solar-wind hybrid systems for remote health centres in the Libyan Sahara Desert, simulated commercial rooftop PV systems for UK university buildings, and conducted full LV network hosting capacity studies using industry-standard grid codes.

I bring hands-on solar PV site engineering experience from Virupaksha Solar Energy. I know what happens between the design office and a live system.
